## English

Two recent reviews have addressed related topics: Eda (2021, *Animal Frontiers*) reviewed the wild ancestry of the chicken and the search for the earliest domestic chicken bones, and Lawal and Hanotte (2021, *Animal Genetics*) reviewed the origin, distribution and adaptation of domestic chickens. Our manuscript differs from these reviews in four respects. First, it incorporates evidence published since 2022 that has substantially revised the timing and routes of chicken domestication and dispersal, including the global zooarchaeological reassessment of Peters et al. (2022), direct radiocarbon dating of early European chicken bones (Best et al., 2022), the debate over early records from Yunnan, and recent genomic studies (2023–2026). Second, it treats domestication as a continuous process extending from initial commensalism and husbandry through dispersal to ongoing gene flow, and uses ancient DNA evidence (e.g., on *TSHR*) to show that some traits now ubiquitous in domestic chickens arose only after dispersal. Third, it evaluates each line of evidence against explicit criteria—species identification, direct dating and domestic status for archaeological remains, and marker type and inference method for genetic data—and summarizes the regional records in a single table. Fourth, it reviews gene flow among red junglefowl, local, commercial and feral chickens, and discusses its consequences for domestication research and for the conservation of wild and local genetic resources, which neither review covers.

## 中文（供核对）

近期有两篇相关综述：Eda（2021，*Animal Frontiers*）回顾了家鸡的野生祖先和最早家鸡骨骼的研究；Lawal 和 Hanotte（2021，*Animal Genetics*）综述了家鸡的起源、分布与适应。本文与二者有四点不同。
1. **纳入2022年以来的新证据**：这些证据大幅修正了家鸡驯化和传播的年代与路线，包括 Peters 等（2022）的全球动物考古重评、欧洲早期鸡骨的直接测年（Best 等，2022）、云南早期记录的争论，以及2023–2026年的基因组研究。
2. **把驯化作为一个连续过程来讨论**：从初始的共栖和饲养、传播，一直到持续至今的基因交流，并用古DNA证据（如 *TSHR*）说明，一些在现代家鸡中普遍存在的性状直到传播后才形成。
3. **按明确的标准评估每类证据**：考古遗存看物种鉴定、直接测年和家养属性；遗传资料看标记类型和推断方式。各地区记录汇总在一张表中。
4. **综述基因交流及其影响**：讨论红原鸡、地方鸡、商业鸡和逸生群体之间的基因交流，以及它对驯化研究和野生、地方遗传资源保护的影响。这两篇综述都没有涉及这部分。
